AlWird Solver

A guessing assistant for the Arabic Wordle game AlWird, built on information theory.

How it works

Every guess splits the remaining candidates into groups, one per colour pattern it could come back with. This tool measures that split in bits of information and ranks the guesses that shrink the field fastest.

It only advises — it never connects to the game and cannot see the answer.

Playing along

  1. Guess on the real site — start with the opener shown at the top of this page.
  2. Type that word into the input box here.
  3. Click the tiles to match the colours you got, then press Submit.
  4. The solver narrows the field and ranks your next guesses. Repeat until you win.

Reading the suggestions

H is the expected information gain in bits — higher is better. One bit halves the remaining candidates, so 5 bits cuts the field by about 32×.

★ marks a word that could still be the answer, so it might win outright. ◇ marks a probe: a word that cannot win, offered because it separates the candidates better.

The settings

Choose Play along, Watch it solve, or Benchmark at the top. Difficulty mirrors the game's hard mode; Advanced holds search depth and the word pool.

Every option has an ⓘ explaining what it changes — hover, tap or focus it.
